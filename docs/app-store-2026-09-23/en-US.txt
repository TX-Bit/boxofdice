PROMOTIONAL TEXT
Roll the dice, close the tiles and aim for zero. Try a quick 9-tile game, Classic, timed challenges, Big Box or multiplayer on one device.

DESCRIPTION
Box of Dice is a simple, engaging dice game based on the classic board game Shut the Box.

Roll the dice, choose tiles that add up to the dice total, and close them. The goal is to leave as few points as possible. Close every tile for a perfect score of 0.

The game is easy to learn, but every roll brings a choice: close one high number or several smaller ones?

Features:
• Classic mode with 12 tiles
• Quick Game with 9 tiles
• Speed Run timed mode
• Big Box with 18 tiles and three dice
• Big Box Speed timed challenge
• Local multiplayer on the same device
• Statistics and personal bests
• Themes and a clean, board-game-inspired look

Box of Dice is perfect for a quick break, relaxed play or beating your personal best.
